**关键词**　颈缩起点、局部应变、宽度变化

高温拉伸中，应变局部化是变形逐渐集中于一段材料的过程。颈缩则涉及局部截面收缩。XTDIC 提供的轴向应变场可以定位候选区域，试样轮廓可以检验该处是否同步变窄，红外热像补充该区域的热历史。这三项观测适合按加载阶段放在同一页读。

## 先找到持续扩大的区域差异

沿标距段划定多个可追踪区域，保持应变定义、拟合尺度和色标一致。观察候选区与邻区之间的应变差是否随加载持续扩大。单个边缘点突然升高时，先检查原图、相关质量和计算窗口是否跨出轮廓。

## 用轮廓给出可复查的起点

在候选区与邻区分别提取横向宽度历史，检查侧向转动和投影变化。若候选区持续积累轴向应变，且经几何核查的轮廓显示该处相对邻区收窄，可用相邻有效帧限定可见颈缩的起始区间。正面轮廓只提供宽度，计算当前截面积还需要厚度测量或明确的模型假设。

## 温度和断裂证据分别怎样使用

把颈缩区温度与邻区及炉内背景对照，讨论局部温升是否伴随变形集中。断裂前散斑可能拉长或剥落，应标出最后有效应变。若需要把表面现象解释为内部孔洞长大或某种断裂机制，应在对应位置做独立的断后检查。

# Identifying Necking Onset in Hot Tension with XTDIC Strain, Specimen Outlines and Thermograms

**Keywords**　Necking onset, local strain, width profile

Strain localization concentrates deformation within part of the gauge. Necking involves a local reduction in cross-section. The XTDIC axial strain field identifies a candidate region, the specimen outline tests for local narrowing, and the infrared record adds its thermal history. Reading them at the same loading stages helps establish when visible necking began.

## Look for sustained divergence

Track several gauge regions under the same strain definition, fitting length and color scale. Examine whether the axial strain difference between the candidate region and its neighbors grows over successive observations. For an isolated edge spike, inspect the original image, correlation quality and whether the fitting window extends outside the specimen.

## Date the onset with the outline

Extract local width histories at the candidate region and its neighbors, checking for rotation and perspective changes. Persistent strain concentration accompanied by verified local narrowing brackets visible necking between neighboring valid frames. A front outline gives width. Estimating the current cross-sectional area also requires thickness information or a declared model assumption.

## Read temperature and fracture evidence

Compare the temperature history at the neck with adjacent material and the furnace background. Report whether local warming accompanies the concentration of deformation. Speckles can stretch or detach near fracture, so identify the last valid strain observation. To attribute the surface signature to internal void growth or a specific fracture mechanism, inspect the corresponding location after fracture.
