**关键词**　高温散斑、表面发射率、红外测温

金属拉伸试样上的黑白散斑服务于可见光相关计算。热像仪对自身工作波段内的辐射敏感。两块区域肉眼看起来反差很大，其红外发射率关系仍需测定。尤其在氧化、涂层开裂或剥落后，热图里新出现的一条亮带值得回到原始照片核查。

## 在加载前检查两路图像

用与正式试样表面工艺一致的见证片，在同一观察窗、视角和加热条件下完成保温检查。稳定时比较纹理不同的邻近区域，并用独立温度参考约束解释。记录红外工作波段、窗口透过和反射背景设置。若差异随表面纹理移动，优先检查发射率设定。涂层本身也可能改变吸热，单凭区域温差不能求出唯一修正量。

## 拉伸时保留表面变化记录

在 XTDIC 双目原图上查看散斑是否仍附着于材料，标出涂层裂开、金属露底的时刻。对同一区域检查热像是否突跳、DIC 是否失去相关。把这些时刻划为需要复核的区间，保留附近表面完整区域作对照。

## 可以交付到什么程度

经过温度参考核验的区域可以输出相应条件下的表面温度历程。仅有热对比的区域可保留为表观温度图，注明表面状态。若待研究的颈缩带恰好落在剥落处，报告应给出最后有效时刻与测温限制，使后续试验能针对该区域改进表面制备。

# Can a High-Temperature Speckle Pattern Change Infrared Readings? Surface Checks for XTDIC Tests

**Keywords**　High-temperature speckles, emissivity, infrared thermometry

A speckle pattern gives visible-light DIC the texture it needs. An infrared camera responds to radiation in its own spectral band. Visible contrast alone does not establish the emissivity contrast of the same marks. Oxidation, coating cracks and spalling are particularly relevant when a new bright band appears during a hot tensile test.

## Check both views before loading

Prepare a witness coupon with the same surface process. Observe it during a dwell through the intended window at the test angle. Compare adjacent textures under stable conditions and use an independent temperature reference. Record the camera band, window transmission and reflected-background settings. If an apparent difference follows the texture, inspect the emissivity assumptions. The coating may also alter heat absorption, so a temperature difference alone cannot determine a unique correction.

## Track surface changes under load

Review both XTDIC images for adhesion of the pattern. Mark when the coating opens or bare metal appears. Inspect the same region for a jump in apparent temperature and for loss of correlation. Flag the affected interval and retain a neighboring intact region as a control.

## Qualify the temperature history

For regions checked against a temperature reference, report the surface temperature under the verified conditions. Label regions supported only by thermal contrast as apparent-temperature observations. If coating loss coincides with the developing neck, record the last usable observation and the limitation. This identifies where surface preparation needs attention in a repeat test.
